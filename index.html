#1 TAKE/STROLL DOWN MEMORY LANE

MeaningExample
To deliberately recall pleasant or sentimental memories.We were just taking a stroll down memory lane and recalling the days of our youth.
To reminisce about the past.Let's go there and take a trip down memory lane.

#2 COMMIT SOMETHING TO MEMORY

MeaningExample
To study something carefully so that you can remember it exactly.She decided to commit the entire acceptance speech to memory.
To memorize perfectly.If you want to pass the test, you must commit all of the equations to memory.

#3 IF MY MEMORY SERVES ME CORRECTLY

MeaningExample
If I remember correctly (used to introduce a potential recollection).I think he was called Sam, if my memory serves me correctly.
A cautious way to confirm information.If my memory serves me correctly, we went to the same high school.

#4 CAN’T GET A WORD IN EDGEWISE

MeaningExample
You can't say anything because someone else is talking so much.Sally got so excited when she was talking that nobody else could get a word in edgewise.
Unable to interject into a conversation.He was talking so much, so I couldn’t get a word in edgewise.

#5 LOSE MY TRAIN OF THOUGHT

MeaningExample
To forget what one was talking or thinking about.I’m sorry, I lost my train of thought. What were we talking about again?
To be momentarily distracted and forget an idea.The question made the speaker lose his train of thought.

#6 LIKE A BROKEN RECORD

MeaningExample
Someone or something that annoyingly repeats itself.He was starting to sound like a broken record, repeating the same advice.
Repetitive and irritating.She’s always complaining about how he treats her. She sounds like a broken record.

#7 CRANK OUT SOMETHING

MeaningExample
To produce something continually, quickly, or mechanically.Stanley can crank out a lot of essays in a single day.
To manufacture or create volume quickly.The new robot they created can crank out over 100 mechanical parts in one hour.

#8 DRIVE A HARD BARGAIN

MeaningExample
Be uncompromising in making a deal; negotiate prices in one's favor.I had to drive a hard bargain with the Korean man at the store.
To negotiate skillfully for one's benefit.You drive a hard bargain, but I’ll sign the contract.

#9 HAVE THE UPPER HAND

MeaningExample
Have or gain an advantage or control over someone or something.We are still waiting to see which party will have the upper hand in the election.
To possess a position of power or control.By half time, the Mexican soccer team seemed to have the upper hand.

#10 HIT A SNAG

MeaningExample
Encounter a problem, obstacle, or unexpected delay.We’ve hit a snag with this design project.
To run into a difficulty.Our project hit a snag, so we had to cancel everything and start over.

#11 ROLL UP ONE’S SLEEVES

MeaningExample
To prepare to fight or work hard.I rolled up my sleeves and exercised every day to meet my goal.
To get ready for serious work.If you roll up your sleeves and work hard, you will succeed one day.

#12 OUT OF THE WOODS

MeaningExample
Out of danger or difficulty; past a critical phase.When the patient got out of the woods, his parents relaxed.
No longer facing the main challenge.The doctor said you would be out of the woods in a week.

#13 ON A SHOESTRING BUDGET

MeaningExample
Having very little money to spend.The movie was produced on a shoestring budget.
With minimal financial resources.We are working on a shoestring budget, so we have to spend money wisely.

#14 BLOW YOUR OWN HORN/TRUMPET

MeaningExample
To tell everyone proudly about your achievements (often used negatively).Stop blowing your own horn. It’s annoying.
To boast or self-promote.I am usually quite humble, but I felt like blowing my own horn today.

#15 BITE THE HAND THAT FEEDS ONE

MeaningExample
Deliberately hurt or offend a benefactor (someone who helps you).How can you bite the hand that feeds you?
To be ungrateful or destructive towards a supporter.He can’t expect sympathy when he bites the hand that feeds him.

#16 DOWN TO EARTH

MeaningExample
Straightforward, practical, and simple in style; modest.Ruth is a very down-to-earth woman.
Realistic and sensible.I like his down-to-earth approach to fixing problems.

#17 BLOW THE WHISTLE

MeaningExample
To report someone’s wrongdoing, illegal activity, or dangerous conditions.The bank manager blew the whistle on the employee's illegal activity.
To expose a secret.He was fired for blowing the whistle on the company’s mismanagement of accounts.

#18 TAKE YOUR HAT OFF TO (SOMEONE)

MeaningExample
It means you admire or respect them, or something they have done.I took my hat off to him for the excellent work he had done.
To show great respect.We have our hats off to him for all that he has accomplished.

#19 HOT SPOT

MeaningExample
A very popular or active place (literal or metaphorical).This restaurant is the hot spot. Everyone is here.
A physical location for internet access (Wi-Fi).Do you know if there is a hotspot here? I need to email someone.

#20 BOOKED SOLID

MeaningExample
To be completely full (in terms of reservations or appointments).The restaurant was booked solid.
To have no more available time for new clients or guests.I went to the hair salon, but the hairdresser said that she was booked solid.

#21 GET SOME R&R

MeaningExample
Rest and Relaxation (abbreviation).I’m really stressed out. I need some R&R.
Time spent on rest and leisure.Last weekend I enjoyed some much needed R&R.

#22 ACQUIRE A TASTE FOR (SOMETHING)

MeaningExample
To develop a desire or preference for a particular food, drink, or experience.He acquired a taste for salad after years of his mom giving it to him.
To start liking something new.They acquired a taste for Mediterranean food after living overseas.

#23 BITTER PILL TO SWALLOW

MeaningExample
Something that is very unpleasant but must be accepted.Losing to my younger sister was a bitter pill to swallow.
An unpleasant fact or disappointment that is difficult to endure.Not passing the exam was a bitter pill to swallow.
